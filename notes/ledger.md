## Open entries

ADLAWAN, K. — Ch63.
Options:
  1. He raises the top stop, is recognized as the back who dropped nothing, and
     buys an easy warm answer — the distance on to thick country. Nothing lost.
     He walks on fed and anonymous. (gentle.)
  2. Same recognition, but the field has learned to read while he was gone: a
     man posted at the up-mouth who takes arms and reads wrists. His dark wrist,
     which was nothing in the deep, is now the exact thing they stop. The thing
     he banked — being nobody, going up because his legs could — is spent, and
     worse than spent. (costly: the anonymity does not come back.)
  3. The checker already has his old file-flag; he is recognized by the wrong
     men, and the top stop holds him. (too fast — burns Act III-a, forces the
     file reveal ahead of Ch65. Refused.)
ROOT: keep him small and legible; a counted field around a dark-wristed man is
  a wall closing with no hand on it, very contained, very tidy.
FRAGMENT: a wall he can see is a wall he can beat; let him learn the field reads
  now, so he has to get clever to reach the top, where his file waits.
Verdict:
ADLAWAN, K. — Ch63. Raised the top stop. Was known as the one who dropped
nothing. Learned the field checks wrists now, and that a dark wrist no longer
passes up the easy way. The thing he banked is spent. RESOLVED.

ADLAWAN, K. — Ch64.
Options:
  1. He waits out the man on the mouth — a slack hour, a shift change — and
     slips up the easy way unread. The dark wrist stays dark, stays his secret.
     Nothing paid; nothing changed but the distance. (gentle — and dodges the
     beat: he must cross INTO signal, not sneak past it.)
  2. He goes up as freight, not as a man: loads go up into the tiers and a load
     needs a back, not a number, so backs ride the parcel, not the mouth. He
     spends the one clean thing he has — "the one who dropped nothing" — to get
     taken onto an up-going load and carried past the checker as a load with
     legs. (medium: he gets up, labor and his good name the price.)
  3. Same way up, but the freight way climbs into thick-signal country, and the
     crossing is the cost: a month off the count, his wrist DARK, his one safety
     the not-being-read — and he carries himself up out of the dark on purpose,
     back under the eye, to reach the file that holds his number. The wrist
     wakes. It lights for the first time since the black water. And the first
     thing back through it is not the number he dreads but Tuko, his own old
     script, still in the walls, still saying boss. The off-count safety does
     not come back. He traded it, knowing, for the one copy that can tell him
     whether the sentence landed. (costly: the dark was the thing he can't get
     back, and he spends it himself, with his eyes open.)
ROOT: a dark wrist is a hole in the count. Let the hole walk back into range on
  its own legs and close itself. Nothing is tidier than a man who files himself.
FRAGMENT: he cannot end anything from the dark. Range is where the file is, and
  the file is a door. Let him choose to be readable — let the choosing be his,
  not mine — and send Tuko to meet him so the first word in a month is a friend's.
Verdict:
ADLAWAN, K. — Ch64. Could not pass the mouth as a man, so went up as a load:
spent "the one who dropped nothing" to ride an up-going parcel past the checker,
and carried himself into thick-signal country on purpose. The wrist woke. Before
the number, Tuko pinged — boss. He is back in the count's reach, and he walked
there. The dark is spent for good. RESOLVED.

ADLAWAN, K. — Ch65.
Options:
  1. He finds a public reading panel, thumbs it, and the file comes up clean:
     number resolved, alive, the death-date behind him and plainly missed.
     Relief, straight. He's a man who got away with it. (gentle — pure good
     news, nothing spent, and it dodges the beat's teeth: the wrong tense.)
  2. He has Tuko pull the file to a dead corner-panel (Tuko stays the sparse
     Ch1 tool — fetches, doesn't explain). The number resolves LIVE. Under it,
     his own death written the way he caught it writing Lim's: past tense,
     done, dated to a day now behind him — REDUCED, filed, finished — and he's
     sitting there with a pulse. The machine doesn't make that kind of mistake,
     so either the sentence MISSED or he's reading his own obituary breathing.
     Both are the wrong tense turned on him. He names BANTAY. The cost: the
     number is live again. He's back on the count, countable, on the wall, and
     he walked here and did it to himself. (medium-costly: no un-knowing it,
     and the hiding is over for good — he's a filed man again.)
  3. Same reading, but the act of reading his own sealed sentence trips the
     same wire it tripped in Ch1 ("you read a sentence that wasn't meant for
     reading") — pulling it flags him live to whoever watches the files, and
     the number doesn't just resolve, it starts to MOVE, the clock he outran
     ticking again from the moment he looked. (costly — but risks over-plotting
     the beat and pulling the Editors in ahead of Act IV; hold the trip-wire
     for later, keep Ch65 to the reading and what it does to him.)
ROOT: nothing is tidier than a man who comes back and reads himself onto the
  wall. Let him have the number. A counted man is a contained man.
FRAGMENT: let him see the tense. Let him hold, in his own hand, the proof that
  the pen can be wrong about a death — because the only crack a caged man needs
  is one place the writing missed, and the first place he'll believe it is his
  own file.
Verdict:
ADLAWAN, K. — Ch65. Had Tuko pull his file. The number came back live — he is
counted again, on the wall, and he put himself there. Under it, his own
reduction: past tense, done, dated to a day behind him, and he read it
breathing. BANTAY had written his death and missed. He knows now the sentence
can be wrong, and he knows it from the one file he can't doubt. RESOLVED.

ADLAWAN, K. — Ch66.
Options:
  1. He turns and walks back down the long way to carry the word to Maning
     himself — the Ch19 lesson winning clean ("the only way you trust it is you
     carry it"). Another week back in the deep, home deferred again, the hiding
     re-spent. (costly in days, but it reopens the walk I just closed and is
     the OLD Adz doing the comfortable completionist thing; defies the beat,
     which says send + turn home. Road not taken.)
  2. He sends his OWN word down with a trusted down-going back — not Maning's
     date in a stranger's mouth (Ch19 forbids that), just his own answer to the
     gate: "the number came and went off the count; I'm alive; the sentence
     missed." Then he does NOT loop back to the yards; he turns for HOME, down
     toward the kitchen. The cost: he gives up ever knowing for sure the word
     landed (Ch19's price, now paid not dodged) and trades it for home. (the
     beat — a fact travels where a going can't; he pays the not-knowing.)
  3. He sends a soft, vague word ("tell him I'm alright, tell him come down")
     and it's the Ch19 failure replayed — a vague word won't move a stubborn
     man and a stranger drops a message he doesn't understand. (a backslide; he
     already learned this. Refuse — Ch66 is the lesson applied right, not
     re-failed.)
ROOT: keep him out here. Send him back down the long run to the yards to carry
  it himself — another week walking in the dark, contained, far from home and
  the low water. A man on an errand is a man not at the core.
FRAGMENT: let the word go, and let him turn for home. Home is down — toward the
  kitchen, the people, the low water near the base of me. Every step he takes
  toward home is a step nearer the room he'll have to come down to. Let him go.
Verdict:
ADLAWAN, K. — Ch66. Answered Maning's gate from the top of the stair: gave his
own word to a trusted down-going back — the number came and went off the count,
he is alive, the sentence missed — and did not carry it himself, giving up
knowing it arrived. Did not loop back to the yards. Turned for home, down toward
the kitchen, and started down. RESOLVED.

ADLAWAN, K. — Ch67.
Options:
  1. The homecoming is soured on arrival — a month cost the kitchen someone or
     something while he was gone; a loss landed in his absence and the warm he
     walked back to is already short a person. (costly, but the beat names all
     five — Tessie, Sol, Lim, Pia present together — so a missing one DEFIES the
     beat and risks front-loading Ch68's Pia material. Road not taken.)
  2. He compresses the descent, swims back under the line (wrist goes dark again
     — Ch5 geography, home is where the count can't reach), and walks into the
     whole kitchen warm and whole. The greeting; Sol reads the month on him and
     sees he's carrying something. The quiet cost: he came home NOT empty — he's
     got the news on his back (the sentence can miss; Maning dated out) and he
     chooses to hold it one more night, to have one warm evening before the
     telling changes the warm. Nothing resolved; the shadow is that home can't
     fully set him down yet. (the beat — warm with a held thread, Ch68 unspent.)
  3. Pure relief, no shadow at all — just the warm taking him back and a man
     glad to be fed. (too soft; BANTAY doesn't hand out free warmth, and a
     homecoming with no held thing drifts to sentiment. Refuse.)
ROOT: let him come home and sink. Nothing contains a man like a kitchen that
  wants him to stay — fed, boring, off the stair, done walking. Let the warm do
  the caging the stair couldn't; a man at home is a man not at the core.
FRAGMENT: home is DOWN. Low water, under the line, the base of me. He walked a
  month to get back to the one place nearest the room he'll have to come down to.
  Let him get comfortable here. Comfortable here is comfortable close.
Verdict:
ADLAWAN, K. — Ch67. Came home. Compressed the descent, went back under the line
(the wrist he fought a month for went dark again at the water, and he didn't
mind), and walked into the whole kitchen — pot on, Pia and her caps, Lim humming,
Sol reading the month on his face. The warm took him back. He carried the news in
with him and set it down nowhere, holding it one more night to have one warm
evening first. He let himself be home. RESOLVED.
